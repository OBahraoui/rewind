# Bulk discount: 10% off orders of 10 or more items

Customers who buy in bulk keep asking for a discount.

**Acceptance criteria**
- Orders with 10 or more items in total get 10% off the subtotal (rounded down to whole cents).
- Orders with fewer than 10 items are unchanged.
- Ship it under new pricing and enable it in staging and production.
- Tests cover 9, 10 and 25 items.
